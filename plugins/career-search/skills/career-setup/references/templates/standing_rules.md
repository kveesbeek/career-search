# Standing rules

The candidate's personal rules for everything written about them: CVs, cover letters, application answers, briefs and interview prep. Skills hold the method; this file holds what is specific to this person and their target roles. When the candidate corrects a fact or a wording rule, update it here (and the narrative if it is a fact), not in a skill.

## Accuracy rules

What may and may not be claimed, beyond the facts in the narrative. Typical entries: deals won by the company versus closed personally; projects led together with someone else; numbers and what they include; methods or tools the candidate knows only by reading.

## Wording rules

- The candidate's writing-style skill applies to everything written as them (see `writing_style_skill` in profile.yaml).
- **Outside readers**: which domain jargon to replace with plain words for readers outside the candidate's domain, and which terms are listed under `cv.insider_terms` in profile.yaml.
- **Small-company readers**: what reads as corporate process to a founder-led company and should be left out or stated once.
- **Money figures against the reader's scale**: which amounts to leave out for small or recently funded companies, and what carries the scale instead.
- **Headline level** matches the posted title.
