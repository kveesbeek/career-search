# Company brief and call prep

Read `00 Sources/profile.yaml` first: conversation language, `candidate.previous_employer` and `candidate.comparison_frame` (what to compare against and what to flag). Read the narrative and `standing_rules.md` for the "why this matters" part.

## Research brief

Objective: a brief that lets the candidate walk into an application, call or interview already informed. Not a rehash of the "About us" page.

**Research order**: the company's own materials first (website, careers page, investor and press pages), then secondary sources. Recent news from the last 6–12 months: funding, leadership changes, product launches, regulatory issues, layoffs, M&A. Look for inconsistencies or red flags the candidate's domain knowledge can spot.

**Structure**:
- **Snapshot**: size, founding year, HQ and locations, ownership. Flag what `comparison_frame` asks to flag.
- **What they do**
- **Customers**
- **Business model**, compared with the previous employer's where relevant.
- **Leadership**: CEO and the leaders the role would work with (background, tenure, stated priorities).
- **Recent news**: dated, with source.
- **Why this matters for the candidate**: 2–4 sentences on strong-fit angles and open questions.

Under one page, with source links for anything from news or leadership commentary. Save as `03 Companies/<Company>/<Company> - Research Brief.md`, then update the pipeline with the career-pipeline-tracker skill (new card with scores if the company isn't on the board yet).

## Call or interview prep

For a named conversation: who the person is (role, background, what they have said publicly), what they are likely to want to find out, the candidate's three or four strongest points for this person with named proof points, the gaps they will probe and how to answer them, and questions for the candidate to ask. Read the research brief and earlier prep in the company folder first. Save as `03 Companies/<Company>/<Company> - <Call type> <Person> (<date>).md`, and update the pipeline (interview date, contact).

## Rules

- Before starting, process answered questions ("Questions inbox" in the career-pipeline-tracker skill).

- Never invent facts about the candidate; use the narrative.
- Cite sources for claims about the company; say when something could not be verified.
